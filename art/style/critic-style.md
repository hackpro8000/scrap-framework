# Style rules: the plain kit

The game's own look rules, read by the critic with `framework/docs/art/critic-rules.md`. Replace them when the game gets its own look.

- Every part is one flat colour from `art/style/palette.luau`. Gradients, noise, mottling or brush marks on a coat are defects.
- Shading is only a light edge highlight and a soft contact shadow where parts meet. Highlights so bright they read as white lines, or shadows that read as black outlines, are defects.
- No wear of any kind: no rust, grime, dust, scratches, chips or drips.
- No warp: faces are flat and edges straight. A bent or dented panel is a defect.
- Colours stay few and plain: greys for structure, one accent per model for what matters, and at most one more for a detail.
- Forms are simple blocks and cylinders with clean, even proportions; detail comes from shape, not surface.
